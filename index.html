Quick answers

AI consulting, explained simply

What does an AI consultant do?

An AI consultant helps a business find where artificial intelligence and automation can solve real problems, then plans and guides the implementation. The work typically covers process review, tool selection, workflow automation, chatbots or assistants, and data reporting, with attention to cost, privacy and measurable outcomes.

What is an AI consulting agency?

An AI consulting agency is a team that provides AI strategy and hands-on implementation as a service, so a business can adopt AI without building a full in-house team. Zaki Enterprises is a global AI consulting agency serving small and growing businesses.

When should a business hire an AI consultant?

Typically when staff spend hours on repetitive tasks, reports take too long, customer questions pile up, or data sits in disconnected tools. See our AI consulting services or book a free consultation.

Can a small business hire an AI consultant?

Yes. Small businesses often benefit most from focused projects, such as automating a repetitive process, adding a website chatbot or building a reporting dashboard, before committing to a larger AI strategy.

How much do AI consulting services cost?

Cost depends on the scope, the tools involved and how much needs to be built. A short discovery conversation usually clarifies the work and lets us suggest a realistic plan before you commit.

What is generative AI consulting?

Generative AI consulting helps businesses use AI models that write, summarize or create content, for example drafting replies, reports or marketing content, with the right guardrails and human review.

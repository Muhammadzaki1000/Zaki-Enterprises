Problems it solves

  • Content planning is slow and ad hoc
  • Posting and reporting take hours
  • Brand voice varies

Who it is for

Marketing agencies, small businesses and online entrepreneurs who publish regularly and want a repeatable process.

Key benefits

  • Faster drafting and planning
  • More consistent brand voice
  • Ideas organized into a content calendar
  • Human approval before anything is published

What you get

  • A brand voice brief
  • Reusable prompts and templates
  • A content planning workflow

Exact deliverables are agreed for each project.

Tools

Tools and technologies

  • AI writing assistance
  • Content calendars in spreadsheets
  • Workflow tools such as n8n
  • Scheduling and analytics exports (where supported)

Workflow

How Social Media Automation works

A typical process, adapted to your project.

  1. 1. Goals

    Define audiences, channels and content aims.

  2. 2. Brief

    Capture brand voice, rules and examples.

  3. 3. Generate

    Draft ideas, copy and assets with AI.

  4. 4. Review

    Edit and approve every piece with a person.

  5. 5. Plan

    Organize approved content into a calendar.

  6. 6. Refine

    Learn from results and improve the prompts.

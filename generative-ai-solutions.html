Generative AI can draft, summarize and answer questions, but it needs the right information and guardrails to be useful at work. FAQ

Generative AI & LLM Solutions: common questions

Will it make things up?

Language models can be wrong. Grounding answers in your documents, showing sources and adding review reduces the risk.

Is my data used to train public models?

We agree which services are used and how data is handled before building, and avoid sharing more than needed.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-augmented generation finds relevant passages in your documents first, then asks the model to answer from them.

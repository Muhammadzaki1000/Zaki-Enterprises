FAQ

n8n Workflow Automation: common questions

What is n8n automation used for?

n8n is a workflow automation platform used to connect apps and automate repetitive tasks, such as moving data between tools, sending notifications and preparing reports.

How is n8n different from Zapier?

Both automate tasks between apps. n8n offers more flexibility, including self-hosting, while Zapier is a hosted service. The better choice depends on your needs and budget.

Do I need my own n8n server?

Options include n8n cloud or self-hosting. We discuss what suits your budget and privacy needs.

Can n8n use AI?

Yes, workflows can call AI models for steps like classifying or drafting, with review where needed.

Is n8n the right tool for every process?

No. We say so when a simpler tool or spreadsheet solves it better.

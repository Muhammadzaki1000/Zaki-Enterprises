Problems it solves

  • Tickets are tagged and routed by hand
  • Customer details live in several tools
  • Agents rewrite similar replies repeatedly

Who it is for

Support and sales teams already using a CRM or help desk who want to reduce repetitive work.

Key benefits

  • Faster first responses
  • Less manual record updating
  • Requests routed to the right person
  • Consistent, reviewable replies

What you get

  • An integration plan for your CRM and help desk
  • Working, tested connections
  • AI-assisted tagging and reply drafts
  • Documentation

Exact deliverables are agreed for each project.

Tools

Tools and technologies

  • Your CRM and help desk (where an API exists)
  • AI language models
  • Webhooks and APIs
  • Workflow tools such as n8n
  • Human approval steps

Final tools depend on your project and the software you already use.

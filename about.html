FAQ

Questions, answered

What services does Zaki Enterprises provide?

AI business automation, AI agents and assistants, chatbots, AI-powered dashboards, content and social media automation, custom integrations, and data analytics.

Can you work with businesses in different countries?

Yes. We are a global brand and work remotely across time zones.

Can AI solutions be customized?

Yes. Every solution is shaped around your workflow, data and goals.

Can AI integrate with existing business applications?

In many cases yes, depending on the applications, their APIs and your security needs.

How long does an AI project take?

It depends on scope, integrations and data. We provide an estimate after discovery.

Do all projects require paid AI APIs?

No. Some solutions use rules, spreadsheets or free tiers. Costs depend on the project.

How can a business request a consultation?

Use the contact form and describe your project.

Is ongoing support available?

Support and improvement options can be agreed per project.

Scope, delivery time, integrations and costs depend on each project’s requirements.

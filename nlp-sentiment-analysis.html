NLP and Sentiment Analysis for Reviews, Messages and Surveys

Customers tell you what they think in reviews, emails, chats and surveys, but nobody has time to read thousands of comments. Natural language processing sorts text by topic and sentiment, highlights urgent issues and turns opinions into numbers you can track. We also check results by hand so you know how far to trust them.

FAQ

NLP & Sentiment Analysis: common questions

Which languages are supported?

That depends on the tools used. We confirm the languages you need before starting.

How accurate is sentiment analysis?

It is useful but imperfect, especially with sarcasm or mixed opinions. We spot-check results and report limits.

What data do you need?

An export of reviews, tickets, emails or survey answers that you are allowed to analyze.
